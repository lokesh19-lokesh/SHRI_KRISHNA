Privacy Policy

How Shri Krishna Dental Hospital protects your personal and healthcare information.

1. Information We Collect

When you schedule an appointment or contact Shri Krishna Dental Hospital (Previously Known as Jo'Dent Dental Clinic), we may collect relevant personal details such as your name, contact phone number, email address, preferred appointment slots, and voluntary dental history details provided during consultation requests.

2. Purpose of Information Use

Any personal or medical information you provide is utilized exclusively for:

  • Scheduling, coordinating, and confirming clinical consultations.
  • Maintaining accurate patient healthcare records.
  • Communicating clinical instructions or follow-up appointment reminders.
  • Verifying scheme eligibility for empanelled programs such as the Employees' Health Scheme (EHS) under Aarogyasree.

3. Medical Confidentiality & Security

We uphold strict medical ethics and data confidentiality standards. Your medical records and clinical diagnostics are treated as confidential healthcare information and will not be sold, leased, or distributed to third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Contacting Our Data Officer

If you have any questions regarding your data privacy or wish to update your contact preferences, please reach out to us at [Hospital Email Address] or call [Hospital Phone Number].
