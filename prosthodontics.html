Prosthodontics, Dental Crowns & Dentures

Engineered tooth replacement using CAD/CAM German Zirconia, 3M Lava, DMLS PFM, and Swiss BPS dentures.

EXCELLENCE IN TOOTH RESTORATION

Customized Tooth Replacement & Full Rehabilitation

Prosthodontics is the specialized dental discipline focused on the diagnosis, treatment planning, and rehabilitation of missing, fractured, or compromised teeth. Led by Dr. K. Sowjanya (Fellowship in Esthetic Facial Surgery, with over 1,000+ completed full-mouth rehabilitations), our department delivers biologically harmonious restorations that balance bite dynamics, chewing efficiency, and lifelike aesthetics.

Whether you require a single protective Zirconia crown after root canal therapy, an aesthetic indirect ceramic veneer, or advanced Swiss BPS complete dentures, our computerized digital scans ensure zero margin of error and maximum longevity.

All premium CAD/CAM Zirconia restorations come backed with official manufacturer warranty certificates (5-year, 10-year, and premium 3M Lava tiers).

Dentures & Removable Prosthetics

Complete Denture (Conventional)

Full-arch acrylic prosthesis restoring complete edentulous jaws with custom-selected acrylic teeth suited to your face shape and natural skin tone.

Complete Denture (Injection)

High-pressure injection molding eliminates polymerization shrinkage, delivering superior suction, density, and void-free resistance to stains and odors.

BPS Denture (Swiss Biofunctional)

Premium Swiss Ivoclar Vivadent standard. Dynamic functional impression techniques replicate your exact jaw movements for unmatched chewing stability.

Flexible Partial Denture (One Jaw)

Made from lightweight, unbreakable biocompatible nylon (Valplast-type). Gum-colored flexible clasps eliminate visible metal hooks completely.

Cast Partial Denture (One Jaw)

Features a rigid, custom-cast medical cobalt-chromium framework with occlusal rests that transfer biting loads safely down the long axis of teeth.

Acrylic Partial Denture (One Jaw)

Economical removable partial denture with wrought-wire clasps, ideal for transitional tooth replacement or budget-friendly tooth restoration.

Clear Night Guard (One Jaw)

Custom thermoformed occlusal splint designed to cushion teeth against nocturnal grinding (bruxism), protecting expensive crowns, veneers, and relieving TMJ tension.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Prosthodontics & Dentures FAQs

Zirconia is 100% metal-free, completely bio-compatible, and will never cause allergic reactions or a black line at the gumline. Furthermore, computerized CAD/CAM fabrication guarantees a precise marginal fit with up to a 10-year warranty.

BPS (Biofunctional Prosthetic System) dentures follow an exacting Swiss protocol that captures individual jaw dynamics during speech and chewing. The result is superior suction retention, realistic tooth aesthetics, and no embarrassing slips during meals.

Flexible partial dentures are made from thermoplastic nylon that bends slightly with oral movement without cracking. They feature pink, gum-shaded clasps that blend invisibly with your oral tissues, eliminating visible metal wire clasps.

Eligible Telangana State Government employees and pensioners can receive cashless or subsidized prosthodontic services as per the prescribed government EHS package guidelines upon card verification at our reception.
