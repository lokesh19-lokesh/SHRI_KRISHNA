Root Canal Treatment (RCT)

Relieve dental pain, eliminate infection, and save your natural tooth.

UNDERSTANDING ENDODONTICS

What Is Root Canal Treatment?

Deep within each tooth lies the dental pulp—a collection of blood vessels, nerves, and connective tissue. When bacteria penetrate the pulp due to deep dental decay, repeated restorations, or tooth trauma, it can cause significant inflammation and localized abscesses.

Root canal treatment involves carefully removing the infected pulp tissue, disinfecting the internal root canals, and sealing them hermetically with biocompatible materials. This eliminates pain, stops bacterial spread into the jawbone, and preserves the natural tooth structure for years to come.

A qualified dental professional can determine whether root canal therapy is suitable for your specific tooth condition.
KEY ADVANTAGES

Benefits of Root Canal Therapy

Relieves Severe Pain

Directly removes infected nerve tissues, ending chronic toothaches and temperature sensitivity.

Saves Natural Tooth

Retaining your biological root prevents adjacent teeth from drifting and preserves your natural bite.

Halts Infection Spread

Prevents bacteria from reaching the periapical jawbone and developing acute dental abscesses.

Restores Full Function

With a final protective crown, you can chew comfortably and with natural confidence.

PROCEDURE STAGES

The Root Canal Treatment Steps

1

Diagnosis & Anesthesia

Digital X-rays evaluate canal anatomy, followed by precise local anesthesia for comfort.

2

Pulp Removal & Cleaning

Infected tissue is gently removed and canals are thoroughly disinfected using rotary instruments.

3

Canal Sealing

Canals are hermetically filled with gutta-percha sealant to prevent bacterial re-entry.

4

Crown Restoration

A durable custom crown is placed to restore structural integrity and normal chewing function.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Root Canal FAQs

A root canal treatment is an endodontic procedure that removes inflamed or infected pulp tissue from inside the tooth, sanitizes the internal canals, and seals them to save the tooth from extraction.

Modern endodontic procedures are performed under effective local anesthesia, making the treatment very manageable and comparable to getting a standard filling. The procedure is aimed at ending the severe pain caused by pulp inflammation.

Removing the infected pulp tissue leaves the natural tooth hollowed and more susceptible to biting fractures. A custom crown provides full structural reinforcement to ensure long-term durability.
