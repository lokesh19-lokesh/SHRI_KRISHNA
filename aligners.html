ESTHETIC ORTHODONTIC CARE

Discreet Teeth Alignment

Clear aligners represent a modern orthodontic treatment designed to correct dental crowding, spacing, and mild-to-moderate malocclusion without the use of metal brackets or wires.

Each set of custom-manufactured trays is worn sequentially for 1 to 2 weeks, progressively moving teeth according to a digitally planned sequence. Because aligners are removable, you can maintain your usual oral hygiene habits and dietary choices with ease.

A dental professional can determine whether clear aligner therapy is suitable for your specific dental alignment.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions About Aligners

Clear aligners apply targeted, calibrated forces to specific teeth at planned intervals. As you transition to new aligner trays every 1-2 weeks, teeth gradually migrate toward their intended positions.

For optimal treatment progression, aligners should be worn consistently for 20 to 22 hours each day, being removed only during meals, drinking hot or sugary liquids, and oral hygiene routines.

While aligners are highly effective for many orthodontic situations, complex skeletal discrepancies or severe rotations may require traditional or hybrid orthodontic techniques. A dental professional can determine whether this treatment is suitable for you.
