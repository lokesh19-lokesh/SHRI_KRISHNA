Orthodontic Treatment

UNDERSTANDING ORTHODONTICS

Proper Alignment for Lifelong Oral Health

Orthodontics is the branch of dentistry dedicated to diagnosing, preventing, and correcting misaligned teeth and improper bite patterns (malocclusions). Correcting dental alignment not only enhances smile aesthetics but also distributes biting forces evenly across the jaw and simplifies plaque removal.

Whether utilizing conventional braces, ceramic appliances, or interceptive orthodontic therapies, our approach focuses on gradual, biologically safe tooth movement supported by accurate diagnostic imaging.

A dental professional can determine whether this treatment is suitable for you after assessing your dental arch and jaw alignment.
KEY ADVANTAGES

Benefits of Orthodontic Correction

Improves Bite Balance

Even alignment prevents disproportionate wear on enamel and reduces strain on temporomandibular joints.

Easier Plaque Removal

Straightened teeth reduce tight food traps, facilitating better brushing and flossing efficiency.

Facial Aesthetics

Properly positioned teeth support natural lip contour and create a balanced facial profile.

Speech & Chewing Clarity

Resolves speech pronunciation hindrances and chewing difficulties caused by severe malocclusions.

TREATMENT JOURNEY

The Orthodontic Care Workflow

1

Clinical Assessment

Diagnostic dental photos, panoramic X-rays, and study models to analyze your bite.

2

Appliance Placement

Careful bonding of brackets or delivery of custom appliances based on your treatment plan.

3

Periodic Adjustments

Scheduled monthly check-ins to monitor movement, change archwires, and fine-tune alignment.

4

Retention Phase

Custom retainers fitted to preserve newly aligned tooth positions permanently.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Orthodontic Treatment FAQs

Orthodontics corrects crowded teeth, excessive spacing, crossbites, overbites, underbites, and open bites, restoring both dental functionality and esthetic symmetry.

An initial screening is frequently conducted around age 7 to evaluate dental eruption and jaw relations. However, orthodontic treatments are highly successful across adolescents and adults of all ages.

Treatment duration typically ranges between 12 and 24 months, depending on the severity of the misalignment, bone response, and patient compliance with maintenance visits.
