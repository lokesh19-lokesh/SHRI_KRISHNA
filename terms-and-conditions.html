Terms & Conditions

Guidelines and terms governing website use and appointment requests.

1. General Educational Information

All content, treatment summaries, and educational materials provided on this website are for informational purposes only. The website content does not constitute formal medical diagnosis, clinical prognosis, or personalized medical advice. A qualified dental professional must examine your oral health in person to formulate an individual treatment plan.

2. Appointment Scheduling & Consultation

Submitting an online appointment form represents a consultation request and is subject to schedule confirmation by our hospital desk. While we make every effort to accommodate your preferred date and time, appointment timings may vary based on clinical emergency procedures and doctor availability.

3. EHS / Scheme Coverage Guidelines

Coverage for dental treatments under the Employees' Health Scheme (EHS) or Aarogyasree is subject to governmental guidelines, active policy eligibility, and required documentation. Patients must produce their valid health cards during registration for official verification.
