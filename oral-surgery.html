Oral Surgery & Wisdom Tooth Removal

Expert management of wisdom tooth impactions, surgical extractions, and minor oral surgery with swift healing.

GENTLE SURGICAL EXCELLENCE

Atraumatic Oral Surgical Procedures

Oral and maxillofacial surgery requires an exceptional combination of clinical precision, detailed anatomical knowledge, and gentle patient handling. At Shri Krishna Dental Hospital, our surgical team prioritizes tooth preservation wherever possible, but when teeth become severely fractured, non-restorable, or painfully impacted, we perform atraumatic removals that preserve maximum surrounding bone.

From routine simple extractions to complex horizontally impacted wisdom teeth, surgical exposure of buried canines, frenectomies, and root-saving apicoectomies with bone grafts, your procedure is conducted in a strictly sterilized operatory under local anesthesia.

Our Oral Surgical Services

All surgical procedures adhere to hospital-grade sterilization protocols with single-use sterile disposables.

Extraction of Tooth (Simple)

Routine removal of severely decayed, loose, or mobile teeth that cannot be restored. Performed atraumatically using specialized dental elevators with minimal bone stress.

Routine & Painless

Extraction of Tooth (Surgical)

Indicated for teeth broken off at the gumline, ankylosed roots, or curved roots. Involves gentle flap reflection, bone relief, and careful root retrieval to preserve ridge volume.

Complex Root Retrieval

Wisdom Tooth Extraction (Upper Jaw)

Removal of problematic upper 3rd molars that cause cheek biting, food traps, sinus pressure, or decay due to inaccessible cleaning angles in the maxillary arch.

Maxillary 3rd Molar

Wisdom Tooth Extraction (Lower Jaw)

Specialized surgical removal of impacted mandibular 3rd molars (mesial, horizontal, or vertical impactions). Relieves recurrent pericoronitis and protects neighboring second molars.

Mandibular Impaction

Impacted Canine / Premolar

Careful removal of deeply buried or ectopic canines and premolars that cannot be realigned orthodontically or are causing root resorption of adjacent sound teeth.

Specialized Impaction

Surgical Exposure of Tooth

Uncovering an unerupted, impacted tooth (such as a maxillary canine) under the gums and attaching an orthodontic bracket with gold chain for guided traction into the dental arch.

Orthodontic Surgical Aid

Frenectomy

Surgical or laser release of a restrictive labial frenum (causing midline gap / diastema) or lingual frenum (ankyloglossia / tongue-tie in children and adults) for speech and bite freedom.

Soft Tissue Release

Apicoectomy (Root Resection)

Microsurgical excision of the infected tip of a tooth root and surrounding periapical cyst where standard root canal retreatment cannot resolve the persistent infection.

Root-Saving Surgery

Apicoectomy (With Bone Graft)

Advanced apical surgery combining root-end resection with biocompatible retrograde filling (MTA) and bone graft placement to rapidly regenerate cortical bone in large periapical bone lesions.

Regenerative Surgery
PATIENT COMFORT & RECOVERY

Smooth Recovery Guidelines

Our surgeons provide clear, printed post-operative instructions along with medication to ensure a fast, painless recovery:

First 24 Hours:

Bite firmly on the sterile gauze pack for 45 minutes. Avoid spitting, smoking, or drinking through straws to protect the blood clot.

Swelling Prevention:

Apply cold compress ice packs intermittently to the cheek during the first 24 hours. Eat soft, cool foods like yogurt, smoothies, and ice cream.

Warm Salt Rinses:

Beginning on Day 2, gently rinse with warm saline water 3–4 times daily after meals to keep the surgical site clean and promote healing.

Prescribed Medication:

Take antibiotics and analgesics strictly as advised by your surgeon to eliminate pain and prevent secondary infection.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Oral Surgery FAQs

Wisdom tooth extraction at Shri Krishna Dental Hospital is completely painless during the procedure due to advanced local anesthetic protocols. Afterward, any mild discomfort is well-controlled with standard pain-relief tablets for 2 to 3 days.

Dry socket (alveolar osteitis) occurs when the protective blood clot in the extraction socket is dislodged prematurely, exposing bone. You can avoid it by not smoking, avoiding straws, avoiding vigorous spitting, and eating soft foods during the first 48 hours.

A simple extraction is done on a tooth visible above the gum line using dental forceps and elevators. A surgical extraction is required when a tooth is broken below the gum line or has impacted, severely curved roots requiring gentle bone relief and sectioning.
