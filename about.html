OUR IDENTITY & MISSION

Excellence in Modern Dental Healthcare

At Shri Krishna Dental Hospital, we believe that high-quality dental care is built upon thorough diagnosis, gentle techniques, and genuine communication with every patient. Our facility is designed to provide a calm, welcoming environment where patients of all ages feel supported.

From preventive examinations and pediatric visits to complex full-mouth rehabilitations with dental implants and digital clear aligners, our treatments are planned with meticulous clinical attention and respect for your oral wellness goals.

Strict Sterilization Hospital-grade hygiene & sanitation standards.
Digital Diagnostics Modern imaging for accurate planning.
CLINICAL PHILOSOPHY

Our Approach to Patient Care

We adhere to evidence-based dental practices, ensuring every recommendation is medically sound, transparent, and aligned with your individual circumstances.

Transparent Consultations

We discuss clinical findings in clear, understandable terms, explaining treatment alternatives so you can make informed healthcare choices.

Conservative Treatment

Whenever possible, we focus on preserving your natural teeth and healthy dental structures through minimally invasive, preventive methods.

Comfortable Setting

Our clinic environment is thoughtfully maintained to alleviate dental stress and provide a reassuring atmosphere for both children and adults.

OFFICIAL SCHEME

Empanelled with Employees' Health Scheme (EHS)

Under Aarogyasree Health Care Trust

As part of our commitment to accessible healthcare, Shri Krishna Dental Hospital serves eligible government employees, pensioners, and dependents under the EHS / Aarogyasree framework for recognized dental treatment protocols.

Please present your official EHS beneficiary health card during registration for eligibility verification and treatment guidance.
