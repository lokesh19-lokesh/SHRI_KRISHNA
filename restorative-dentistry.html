BIOMIMETIC CAVITY REPAIR & TOOTH PRESERVATION

Restorative Dentistry & Dental Fillings

Seamless, tooth-colored composite restorations and direct aesthetic veneers that restore natural strength.

CONSERVATIVE DENTISTRY

Preserving Natural Tooth Structure

Restorative dentistry focuses on diagnosing, treating, and repairing teeth damaged by dental caries (decay), trauma, acid erosion, or mechanical wear. Gone are the days of unsightly black mercury amalgam fillings. Today, our clinicians practice biomimetic dentistry—using modern nanohybrid composite resins and glass ionomers that replicate the mechanical strength, flexural modulus, and optical translucency of natural enamel and dentin.

Each restoration is placed under clean isolation, with high-definition digital RVG Radiographs used to verify complete decay elimination and tight, smooth contact contours between adjacent teeth.

Prompt restoration of small cavities prevents deep infection from reaching the nerve pulp, saving you from more extensive root canal treatment.
CLINICAL TARIFF & PROCEDURES

Restoration Options (Per Tooth)

Our restorative offerings cover diagnostic imaging, temporary pulp-soothing dressings, and permanent aesthetic restorations.

Professional Consultation & RVG

Comprehensive oral examination paired with ultra-low radiation digital RVG (RadioVisioGraphy) X-rays. Instant on-screen visualization detects hidden interdental decay.

Diagnostic Foundation

ZOE Restoration (Sedative Dressing)

Zinc Oxide Eugenol temporary filling with active eugenol oil properties that calm inflamed dental pulp in deep cavities, relieving sensitivity before permanent restoration.

Pulp-Soothing Temporary

GIC Restoration

Glass Ionomer Cement filling that forms a direct chemical bond with tooth enamel and dentin while continually releasing fluoride to prevent recurrent secondary cavities.

Fluoride-Releasing Protection

Composite Restoration (Class I)

Tooth-colored light-cured composite resin for cavities located on the biting (occlusal) surfaces of molars and premolars. Matches your natural tooth shade with zero metal.

Occlusal Tooth-Colored

Composite Restoration Class II

Restoration of cavities involving the interdental contact surfaces between adjacent back teeth. Rebuilt with sectional matrix bands to prevent food impaction and floss shredding.

Interproximal Rebuilding

Composite Class III & Class IV

Aesthetic anterior tooth restorations repairing proximal decay and chipped or broken incisal edges. Multi-shade layering mimics natural enamel translucency seamlessly.

Anterior Smile Repair

Direct Composite Veneer

Chairside cosmetic transformation crafted in a single visit. High-polish nanohybrid resin is sculpted over front teeth to close midline gaps (diastemas), cover intrinsic stains, and reshape uneven edges at a fraction of porcelain veneer cost.

Same-Day Smile Makeover
GENTLE RESTORATION PROCESS

How We Restore Your Teeth

1

Digital Diagnosis

RVG radiograph and visual examination map the exact depth and extent of decay.

2

Decay Removal

Gentle, conservative cavity excavation under local anesthesia removes infected softened enamel.

3

Bonding & Layering

High-strength adhesive is applied, followed by shade-matched composite increments cured with LED light.

4

Occlusion & Polish

The bite is checked with articulating paper, followed by diamond paste polishing for a glass-like finish.
