RESTORATIVE DENTAL CARE

Dental Implants: Permanent Tooth Replacement

Dental implants are designed to serve as durable, artificial tooth roots that anchor custom prosthetics such as crowns, bridges, or dentures. When a natural tooth is lost due to injury or decay, dental implants help maintain jawbone integrity and prevent adjacent teeth from shifting.

  • Helps restore natural biting and chewing efficiency.
  • Stimulates underlying jawbone to reduce bone resorption over time.
  • Custom-shaded crowns designed to match surrounding teeth seamlessly.

MODERN ORTHODONTICS

Clear Aligners: Discreet Teeth Straightening

Clear aligners provide a nearly invisible, removable approach to correcting mild-to-moderate dental misalignment, spacing, and crowding. Made of medical-grade thermoplastic material, each tray gently guides teeth into planned positions.

Invisalign Spark Aligners In-house Aligners
  • Removable trays allow easy brushing, flossing, and unhindered eating.
  • Smooth plastic construction without metal wires or sharp brackets.
  • Digital 3D simulation to visualize projected alignment milestones.
