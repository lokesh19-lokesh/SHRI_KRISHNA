Dr. Dedeepya Kandregula

Bachelor of Dental Surgery (BDS) | 4+ Years of Professional Clinical Experience in Dentistry

"Dedicated to Healthy Smiles, Comfortable Care & Confident Patients"

Dr. Dedeepya Kandregula is a qualified dental professional with a Bachelor of Dental Surgery (BDS) degree and over 4 years of clinical experience in dentistry. She is committed to providing comprehensive dental care with a focus on prevention, accurate diagnosis, effective treatment, and long-term oral health.

She believes that dentistry goes beyond treating an immediate dental concern. By combining clinical knowledge, practical experience, clear communication, and compassionate care, she helps patients understand their dental needs and make informed decisions about their oral health.

With experience in managing a wide range of general dental concerns, Dr. Dedeepya follows a professional approach centred on careful assessment, appropriate treatment planning, and preventive care. She places importance on early intervention and patient education to help individuals maintain good oral hygiene and address dental concerns at the right time.
