CARING FOR GROWING SMILES

Pediatric Dentistry

Friendly, gentle, and positive dental experiences for infants, children, and teenagers.

CHILD-CENTERED CARE

Gentle Oral Healthcare for Children

A child's early dental experiences shape their lifelong attitude toward oral hygiene. At Shri Krishna Dental Hospital, our pediatric care focuses on establishing positive, comforting relationships with young patients while monitoring dental arch growth and tooth eruption.

From preventive fluoride applications and dental sealants to gentle restoration of primary teeth, our clinical team emphasizes prevention, patient education, and a calming approach that puts both children and parents at ease.

A qualified dental professional can determine whether this treatment is suitable for your child's specific dental stage.
PREVENTIVE FOCUS

Pediatric Dental Services

Dental Sealants

Protective resin coatings applied to molars to guard deep grooves against food accumulation.

Fluoride Applications

Professional topical fluoride treatments to strengthen developing enamel and resist acids.

Space Maintainers

Preserving space in the dental arch when a baby tooth is lost prematurely.

Oral Hygiene Education

Fun, easy-to-understand brushing and dietary guidance tailored for young children.

PARENT FAQS

Frequently Asked Questions

Dental associations recommend an initial check-up when the first primary tooth erupts, or around a child's first birthday. Early visits help establish familiarity and allow timely preventive monitoring.

Primary teeth are critical for healthy speech development, proper chewing nutrition, and reserving exact spacing in the jaw for incoming adult teeth.

Frame the dental appointment as a positive, routine adventure. Avoid using words like "pain" or "needles", and allow our friendly staff to guide the child gently through their exam.
